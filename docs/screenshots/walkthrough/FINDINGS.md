## 3. Not judged

- **Registration birth details form and the Hindi message.** Never shown (F-07).
- **Charts, Remedies and Mantras content.** `MyBiodata` and `MyContent` are not stubbed, so only error states were seen (F-10).
- **Right sidebar on phone.** It could not be opened (F-01).
- **End of the phone landing page, including its footer** (F-32 b). The desktop footer was read.
- **Lower part of phone pricing and Profile**, partly hidden by the white band (F-32 a).
- **Reading quality, dates and astrological values.** All stand-in text.
- **Real server behaviour.** Quota enforcement, two-tab races, region detection from CloudFront and saved plans were not exercised. Only price display was checked, and it matches the billing doc in both currencies.
- **Paid plan states.** Plus and Unlimited accounts, the Plus limit message and the paid menu were not in the run.
- **Phone keyboard, real touch and screen readers.** Not emulated.
- **Entrance animations.** Faded sections were caught mid-animation and not counted as contrast bugs.

Checked, nothing to report: "Radha", "pyjhora" and "jhora" appear in no screenshot and nowhere in `src`. Manifests show 0 horizontal overflow and no failed requests on any step. The chat page does not scroll as a page, and its header and prompt bar stay fixed.
